Namaz e Janazah (Funeral Prayer)

Salat al-Janazah — the complete method

Introduction

Salat al-Janazah is a prayer of supplication and intercession for the deceased. Unlike the daily prayers, it contains none of the following:

✕ Adhan
✕ Iqamah
✕ Ruku' (bowing)
✕ Sujud (prostration)
✕ Tashahhud
✕ Salam

It consists of five Takbirs (Allahu Akbar) with specific recitations between them.

Before the Prayer

1. Preparation of the Deceased

The deceased should be:

  • Ghusl (ritually washed)
  • Kafan (shrouded)
  • Placed facing the Qiblah

2. Arrangement

  • The congregation forms rows behind the Imam.
  • The body is placed in front of the congregation.
  • The Imam stands near the chest of a male deceased, or near the middle of a female deceased.

Niyyah (Intention)

Silently intend:

"I offer Salat al-Janazah seeking nearness to Allah and praying for the forgiveness of this deceased."

No verbal intention is required.

The Five Takbirs

Each Takbir pairs an obligatory (wajib) line — the minimum required to fulfil the prayer — with a longer recommended (mustahab) version. Either may be recited; the mustahab wording is simply the fuller, more complete supplication.

1

First Takbir

Say اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ Allahu Akbar
Obligatory أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَأَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ اللَّهِ Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah wa anna Muhammadan Rasulullah.

"I witness that there is no god but Allah, and I witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah."

Mustahab — recommended أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا عَبْدُهُ وَرَسُولُهُ أَرْسَلَهُ بِالْحَقِّ بَشِيرًا وَنَذِيرًا بَيْنَ يَدَيِ السَّاعَةِ Ashhadu an la ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh, arsalahu bil-haqqi bashiran wa nadhiran bayna yadayis-sa'ah.

"I bear witness that there is no god except Allah alone, without partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger, sent by Him in truth as a bearer of glad tidings and a warner before the Hour."

2

Second Takbir

Say اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ Allahu Akbar
Obligatory اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa Aali Muhammad.

"O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and the Family of Muhammad."

Mustahab — recommended اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ وَارْحَمْ مُحَمَّدًا وَآلَ مُحَمَّدٍ كَأَفْضَلِ مَا صَلَّيْتَ وَبَارَكْتَ وَتَرَحَّمْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَآلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ وَصَلِّ عَلَى جَمِيعِ الْأَنْبِيَاءِ وَالْمُرْسَلِينَ Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa Aali Muhammad, warham Muhammadan wa Aala Muhammad, ka'afdali ma sallayta wa barakta wa tarahhamta 'ala Ibrahima wa Aali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidun Majid, wa salli 'ala jami'il-anbiya'i wal-mursalin.

"O Allah, bless Muhammad and the Family of Muhammad, and have mercy on Muhammad and the Family of Muhammad, as the best of what You blessed, favoured, and showed mercy to Ibrahim and the Family of Ibrahim — You are Praiseworthy, Glorious. And send blessings upon all the prophets and messengers."

3

Third Takbir

Say اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ Allahu Akbar
Obligatory اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ Allahummaghfir lil-mu'minina wal-mu'minat.

"O Allah, forgive the believing men and women."

Mustahab — recommended اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِلْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ وَالْمُسْلِمِينَ وَالْمُسْلِمَاتِ الْأَحْيَاءِ مِنْهُمْ وَالْأَمْوَاتِ تَابِعْ بَيْنَنَا وَبَيْنَهُمْ بِالْخَيْرَاتِ إِنَّكَ مُجِيبُ الدَّعَوَاتِ إِنَّكَ عَلَى كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ Allahummaghfir lil-mu'minina wal-mu'minati wal-muslimina wal-muslimati al-ahya'i minhum wal-amwat, tabi' baynana wa baynahum bil-khayrat, innaka mujibud-da'awat, innaka 'ala kulli shay'in qadir.

"O Allah, forgive the believing men and women, the Muslim men and women, the living among them and the deceased. Join us with them in good. You answer supplications. You have power over all things."

4

Fourth Takbir

Say اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ Allahu Akbar
Obligatory — for a male اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِهَٰذَا الْمَيِّتِ Allahummaghfir li-hadhal-mayyit.

"O Allah, forgive this deceased."

Obligatory — for a female اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِهَٰذِهِ الْمَيِّتَةِ Allahummaghfir li-hadhihil-mayyitah.

"O Allah, forgive this deceased."

Mustahab — recommended, for a male اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّ هَٰذَا عَبْدُكَ وَابْنُ عَبْدِكَ وَابْنُ أَمَتِكَ نَزَلَ بِكَ وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ مَنْزُولٍ بِهِ اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا لَا نَعْلَمُ مِنْهُ إِلَّا خَيْرًا وَأَنْتَ أَعْلَمُ بِهِ مِنَّا اللَّهُمَّ إِنْ كَانَ مُحْسِنًا فَزِدْ فِي إِحْسَانِهِ وَإِنْ كَانَ مُسِيئًا فَتَجَاوَزْ عَنْهُ وَاغْفِرْ لَهُ اجْعَلْهُ عِنْدَكَ فِي أَعْلَى عِلِّيِّينَ وَاخْلُفْ عَلَى أَهْلِهِ فِي الْغَابِرِينَ وَارْحَمْهُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ يَا أَرْحَمَ الرَّاحِمِينَ Allahumma inna hadha 'abduka wabnu 'abdika wabnu amatika, nazala bika wa anta khayru manzulin bih. Allahumma inna la na'lamu minhu illa khayran wa anta a'lamu bihi minna. Allahumma in kana muhsinan fazid fi ihsanihi, wa in kana musi'an fatajawaz 'anhu waghfir lah. Ij'alhu 'indaka fi a'la 'illiyyin, wakhluf 'ala ahlihi fil-ghabirin, warhamhu birahmatika ya arhamar-rahimin.

"O Allah, this is Your servant, son of Your servant and Your maidservant. He has come to You, and You are the best one to be come to. O Allah, we know nothing of him but good, and You know him better than we do. O Allah, if he was righteous, increase his righteousness, and if he erred, overlook his faults and forgive him. Place him with You in the highest 'Illiyyin, be a successor to his family he leaves behind, and have mercy on him with Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful."

Mustahab — recommended, for a female اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّ هَٰذِهِ أَمَتُكَ وَابْنَةُ عَبْدِكَ وَابْنَةُ أَمَتِكَ نَزَلَتْ بِكَ وَأَنْتَ خَيْرُ مَنْزُولٍ بِهَا اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا لَا نَعْلَمُ مِنْهَا إِلَّا خَيْرًا وَأَنْتَ أَعْلَمُ بِهَا مِنَّا اللَّهُمَّ إِنْ كَانَتْ مُحْسِنَةً فَزِدْ فِي إِحْسَانِهَا وَإِنْ كَانَتْ مُسِيئَةً فَتَجَاوَزْ عَنْهَا وَاغْفِرْ لَهَا اجْعَلْهَا عِنْدَكَ فِي أَعْلَى عِلِّيِّينَ وَاخْلُفْ عَلَى أَهْلِهَا فِي الْغَابِرِينَ وَارْحَمْهَا بِرَحْمَتِكَ يَا أَرْحَمَ الرَّاحِمِينَ Allahumma inna hadhihi amatuka wabnatu 'abdika wabnatu amatika, nazalat bika wa anta khayru manzulin bih. Allahumma inna la na'lamu minha illa khayran wa anta a'lamu biha minna. Allahumma in kanat muhsinatan fazid fi ihsaniha, wa in kanat musi'atan fatajawaz 'anha waghfir laha. Ij'alha 'indaka fi a'la 'illiyyin, wakhluf 'ala ahliha fil-ghabirin, warhamha birahmatika ya arhamar-rahimin.

"O Allah, this is Your maidservant, daughter of Your servant and Your maidservant. She has come to You, and You are the best one to be come to. O Allah, we know nothing of her but good, and You know her better than we do. O Allah, if she was righteous, increase her righteousness, and if she erred, overlook her faults and forgive her. Place her with You in the highest 'Illiyyin, be a successor to her family she leaves behind, and have mercy on her with Your mercy, O Most Merciful of the merciful."

5

Fifth Takbir

Say اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ Allahu Akbar

The prayer is complete. There is no Salam.

The Obligatory Form at a Glance

The obligatory (wajib) wording from each Takbir above, gathered in one place — this alone is sufficient to fulfil the prayer:

TakbirSayThen recite
First Allahu Akbar Ashhadu an la ilaha illallah wa anna Muhammadan Rasulullah
Second Allahu Akbar Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa Aali Muhammad
Third Allahu Akbar Allahummaghfir lil-mu'minina wal-mu'minat
Fourth Allahu Akbar Male: Allahummaghfir li-hadhal-mayyit Female: Allahummaghfir li-hadhihil-mayyitah (O Allah, forgive this deceased.)
Fifth Allahu Akbar Prayer ends.

If the Deceased Is a Child

A special supplication is recited asking Allah to make the child a source of reward and intercession for the parents.

Etiquettes After the Prayer

After completing Salat al-Janazah it is recommended to:

  • Join the funeral procession if possible.
  • Participate in the burial.
  • Recite Surah al-Fatihah for the deceased.
  • Recite Salawat abundantly.
  • Pray for all Marhumeen.

A commonly recited dua is:

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لَهُ وَارْحَمْهُ وَعَافِهِ وَاعْفُ عَنْهُ Allahummaghfir lahu warhamhu wa 'afihi wa'fu 'anhu "O Allah, forgive him, have mercy on him, grant him well-being, and pardon him."

For a female, replace lahu with laha.

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa Aali Muhammad الْفَاتِحَةُ لِرُوحِ جَمِيعِ الْمُؤْمِنِينَ وَالْمُؤْمِنَاتِ خُصُوصًا آبَائِنَا وَأُمَّهَاتِنَا وَمَرَاجِعِنَا وَعُلَمَائِنَا وَشُهَدَائِنَا 🤲🏻 Al-Fatiha li-ruhi jami'il-mu'minina wal-mu'minat, khususan aba'ina wa ummahatina wa maraji'ina wa 'ulama'ina wa shuhada'ina. "Al-Fatiha for the souls of all believing men and women — especially our fathers, our mothers, our maraji', our scholars, and our martyrs."
