Namaz e Janazah (Funeral Prayer)

Salat al-Janazah — the complete method

Introduction

Salat al-Janazah is a prayer of supplication and intercession for the deceased. Unlike the daily prayers, it contains none of the following:

✕ Adhan
✕ Iqamah
✕ Ruku' (bowing)
✕ Sujud (prostration)
✕ Tashahhud
✕ Salam

It consists of five Takbirs (Allahu Akbar) with specific recitations between them.

Before the Prayer

1. Preparation of the Deceased

The deceased should be:

  • Ghusl (ritually washed)
  • Kafan (shrouded)
  • Placed facing the Qiblah

2. Arrangement

  • The congregation forms rows behind the Imam.
  • The body is placed in front of the congregation.
  • The Imam stands near the chest of a male deceased, or near the middle of a female deceased.

Niyyah (Intention)

Silently intend:

"I offer Salat al-Janazah seeking nearness to Allah and praying for the forgiveness of this deceased."

No verbal intention is required.

The Five Takbirs

1

First Takbir

Say الله أكبر Allahu Akbar
Then recite — Shahadatayn أشهد أن لا إله إلا الله وحده لا شريك له وأشهد أن محمدًا عبده ورسوله Ashhadu an la ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh.

"I bear witness that there is no god except Allah alone without partner, and I bear witness that Muhammad is His servant and Messenger."

2

Second Takbir

Say الله أكبر Allahu Akbar
Then recite — Salawat اللهم صل على محمد وآل محمد Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa Aali Muhammad

"O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and the Family of Muhammad."

3

Third Takbir

Say الله أكبر Allahu Akbar
Then pray for all believers اللهم اغفر للمؤمنين والمؤمنات والمسلمين والمسلمات الأحياء منهم والأموات Allahummaghfir lil-mu'minina wal-mu'minat wal-muslimina wal-muslimat al-ahya'i minhum wal-amwat.

"O Allah, forgive the believing men and women, the Muslim men and women, the living among them and the deceased."

4

Fourth Takbir

Say الله أكبر Allahu Akbar
Then pray specifically for the deceased — for a male اللهم إن هذا عبدك وابن عبدك وابن أمتك نزل بك وأنت خير منزول به، اللهم إنا لا نعلم منه إلا خيراً وأنت أعلم به منا، اللهم إن كان محسناً فزد في إحسانه وإن كان مسيئاً فتجاوز عن سيئاته واغفر له وارحمه Allahumma inna hadha 'abduka wabnu 'abdika wabnu amatika, nazala bika wa anta khayru manzulin bih. Allahumma inna la na'lamu minhu illa khayran wa anta a'lamu bihi minna. Allahumma in kana muhsinan fazid fi ihsanihi, wa in kana musi'an fatajawaz 'an sayyi'atihi waghfir lahu warhamhu.

"O Allah, this is Your servant and the son of Your servant and Your maidservant. He has come before You, and You are the best host. We know nothing of him except good, and You know him better than we do. If he was righteous, increase him in righteousness, and if he erred, overlook his faults. Forgive him and have mercy on him."

For a female: replace هذا عبدك with هذه أمتك, and له with لها.
5

Fifth Takbir

Say الله أكبر Allahu Akbar

The prayer is complete. There is no Salam.

A Shorter Common Version

Many believers recite the minimum recommended form:

TakbirSayThen recite
First Allahu Akbar Shahadatayn
Second Allahu Akbar Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa Aali Muhammad
Third Allahu Akbar Allahummaghfir lil-mu'minina wal-mu'minat
Fourth Allahu Akbar Allahummaghfir lihadha al-mayyit(O Allah, forgive this deceased.)
Fifth Allahu Akbar Prayer ends.

If the Deceased Is a Child

A special supplication is recited asking Allah to make the child a source of reward and intercession for the parents.

Etiquettes After the Prayer

After completing Salat al-Janazah it is recommended to:

  • Join the funeral procession if possible.
  • Participate in the burial.
  • Recite Surah al-Fatihah for the deceased.
  • Recite Salawat abundantly.
  • Pray for all Marhumeen.

A commonly recited dua is:

اللهم اغفر له وارحمه وعافه واعف عنه Allahummaghfir lahu warhamhu wa 'afihi wa'fu 'anhu "O Allah, forgive him, have mercy on him, grant him well-being, and pardon him."

For a female, replace lahu with laha.

اللهم صل على محمد وآل محمد Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa Aali Muhammad الفاتحة لروح جميع المؤمنين والمؤمنات خصوصاً آبائنا وأمهاتنا ومراجعنا وعلمائنا وشهدائنا 🤲🏻
