Understanding Adhan and Iqamah

The Call to Prayer and the Immediate Call to Stand for Prayer

Introduction

Prayer (Salah) is the central act of worship in Islam and serves as a direct connection between the servant and Allah. Before each obligatory prayer, two beautiful proclamations are traditionally recited: Adhan and Iqamah.

Adhan is the public call that announces the time of prayer and invites believers toward worship, success, and the remembrance of Allah.

Iqamah is recited immediately before the prayer begins. It contains many of the same phrases as the Adhan but serves as a final announcement that the congregation is about to stand and commence the prayer.

Together, they remind believers of the oneness of Allah, the prophethood of Muhammad ﷺ, the importance of prayer, and the path to true success in this life and the Hereafter.

Adhan

The Adhan is a declaration of faith and an invitation to worship. It is often recited from the mosque to inform the community that the time for prayer has entered.

The words of the Adhan emphasize:

  • The greatness of Allah
  • The Oneness of Allah
  • The message of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ
  • Loyalty to divine guidance
  • The invitation to prayer
  • The invitation to success
  • The importance of prayer as the best of deeds

Iqamah

The Iqamah is recited immediately before the prayer begins. It serves as a final summons for those present to stand in readiness for worship.

While it closely resembles the Adhan, it is shorter and contains the additional phrase:

قَدْ قَامَتِ الصَّلَاةُ Qad Qamatis-Salah which announces that the prayer has now commenced

Meanings at a Glance

A short summary (Ma'roof Tafsir-e-Mafhoom, Mukhtasar) of what each phrase asks of the believer.

Allahu Akbar

Allah is greater than everything — above all else.

Allah har cheez se bara aur buland hai

La ilaha illallah

The right to worship and servitude belongs to Allah alone.

Ibadat aur bandagi ka haq sirf Allah ko hai

Muhammadan Rasulullah

Allah conveyed His guidance to humanity through His Messenger, Muhammad ﷺ.

Allah ne hidayat apne Rasool Muhammad ﷺ ke zariye insaniyat tak pohanchayi

Aliyyan Waliyyullah

Imam Ali (ع) is the Wali of Allah — the successor (wasi) and guide of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ.

Imam Ali (ع) Allah ke Wali aur Rasool Allah ﷺ ke wasi aur rehnuma hain

Hayya alas-Salah

An invitation to prayer.

Namaz ki taraf da'wat

Hayya alal-Falah

An invitation to success in this world and the Hereafter.

Duniya aur Akhirat ki kamyabi ki taraf da'wat

Hayya ala Khayril-'Amal

Prayer is the most excellent and best of deeds.

Namaz sab se afzal aur behtareen amal hai

Qad Qamatis-Salah

The prayer has now begun.

Namaz ab shuru ho chuki hai

Conclusion

The Adhan and Iqamah are not merely announcements of prayer times; they are concise declarations of faith, worship, guidance, and success. Every phrase carries a profound spiritual message that prepares the believer's heart to stand before Allah with awareness, sincerity, and devotion.

اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَآلِ مُحَمَّدٍ Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammadin wa Aali Muhammad O Allah, send Your blessings upon Muhammad and the Family of Muhammad.

Special Note Regarding Funeral Prayers (Salat al-Janazah)

Unlike the five daily prayers, Salat al-Janazah is performed without Adhan and Iqamah. The congregation gathers directly for the prayer, which consists of a series of Takbirs and supplications seeking Allah's mercy, forgiveness, and blessings for the deceased.
