Resources · Asma ul Husna

The 99 Beautiful Names of Allah

Asma ul Husna (أسماء الله الحسنى) — the Most Beautiful Names — are the attributes by which Allah has described Himself in the Qur'an and through the Ahlul Bayt (AS). Each one is a doorway: to know the Name is to begin to reflect it.

"And to Allah belong the Most Beautiful Names, so call upon Him by them." Surah Al-A'raf, 7:180
99 names Calligraphy · Romanized Urdu · English · Meaning Flashcards to help you memorize

All 99 Names

Each card shows the name in Arabic calligraphy, its Romanized Urdu invocation ("Yā …"), the English transliteration and meaning. Open a card for its Qur'anic reference and the traditionally reported benefit of reciting it.
