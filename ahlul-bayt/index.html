Ahlul Bayt

The Fourteen Masoomeen

Chaudah Masoomeen (AS) — Rasool Allah (SAWW), Sayeda Fatima al-Zahra (SA), and the Twelve Imams (AS). The first five, the Panjatan Pak, were gathered beneath the Cloak (Hadith al-Kisa) when the Verse of Purification was revealed.

"Allah only desires to keep away impurity from you, O Ahlul Bayt, and to purify you with a thorough purification." Surah Al-Ahzab, 33:33
